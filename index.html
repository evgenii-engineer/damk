A visual world that feels forgotten, but familiar.

My name is DAMK. I make paintings, characters and objects from things I remember — and things I never want to forget.

I grew up in a post-Soviet city where garages, playgrounds, cracked tiles and old cars meant safety. Now I live in Lisbon and I rebuild those scenes — sometimes in paint, sometimes in plastic, sometimes as a small monkey figure that follows me everywhere.

I work with memory, loneliness, and everyday urban silence. My pieces live somewhere between street culture and soft sadness.

The places that shaped us, even if nobody noticed them. My practice mixes nostalgia, street memory, irony and quiet objects — like playgrounds without kids, or toys that never smile.
